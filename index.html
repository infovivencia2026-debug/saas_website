01Admissions

A new admission should create a student. Not more data entry.

The entire funnel from first enquiry to enrollment — leads, applications, entrance tests, interviews, offers, documents and campaigns. Once admitted, what was collected becomes the student's institutional record.

  • Leads and applications

    Walk-ins, calls and online forms in one funnel, with follow-ups that do not get lost.

  • Tests, interviews, offers

    Entrance scores, interview panels and offer letters against the same application.

  • Documents and enrollment

    The checklist that turns an offer into a roll number, a section and a fee plan.

The office finishes the admission. The rest of the institution can begin.

03Attendance

Attendance should tell you more than who was absent.

Mark it period by period, track late arrivals, handle corrections and connect attendance devices — then see the patterns before they become problems.

  • Period and daily marking

    Student and faculty attendance, marked where the teacher already is.

  • Devices and corrections

    Biometric and RFID feeds, with a correction trail rather than an overwrite.

  • Patterns, not totals

    Late arrivals, early departures and the absences that are becoming a habit.

A pattern you discover today is more useful than a report you discover next month.

What exists right now

The interface layer, complete and clickable: routing, tabs, drawers, modals, search, filter, multi-column sort, pagination, row selection, bulk actions, form validation and drag-and-drop boards. Every screen in this page is real.

What a production deployment adds

Database and APIs behind the registry · Authentication · Permission enforcement · Payment gateways · Messaging · Biometric and RFID systems · Production integrations.

Why we say so

We'd rather show you exactly what exists than hide behind a roadmap. Everything above is demonstrable in ten minutes, and the parts that are not built yet are named rather than implied.
